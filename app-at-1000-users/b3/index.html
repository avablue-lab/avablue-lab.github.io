The model: one server, one Postgres with default settings (max_connections = 100). Every page load is one query. Each person loads a page, reads it for 5–15 s, then loads the next one. Postgres runs up to 100 queries at once; every other request waits in line for a free connection. With the switch on, a person who has waited 5 s since their last click clicks refresh: their old request stays in line and still runs, but nobody is waiting for it anymore. The auto tour: 50 people for 3 s, 1,000 people from 3 s to 13 s, then 50 people until 30 s. URL: #t=12.5 draws that moment of the tour (deterministic), &refresh=0|1 sets the switch, &q=500 sets the query time in ms.
