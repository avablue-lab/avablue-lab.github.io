The prompt
Make me a tiny pixel-art game in a single index.html, no libraries and no external files: a paper plane flies through a neon city at night, you tap or press space to climb, and you dodge the rooftops and antennas. Show a score, and restart after a crash. Also add a demo mode: when the URL has #t=<seconds>, a simple autopilot plays and the page draws exactly that moment of the demo (seeded random, no clock), so I can record it frame by frame. Before you finish, look at your game: render frames of the demo with `python3 -I tools/screenshot.py <your index.html> <out.png> --hash t=<seconds>` at a few different moments, open the PNGs and look at them, and fix anything that looks wrong.
