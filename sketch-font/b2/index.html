Tips for a clean result
  • Use a dark felt pen or marker on plain white paper, in even light with no shadows.
  • Leave clear space between letters and between rows, and don't let letters touch.
  • Keep the paper flat and the photo straight on. Crop away the table and the paper edges if you can.
  • If letters break apart or blobs appear, move the ink sensitivity slider and the font rebuilds.
